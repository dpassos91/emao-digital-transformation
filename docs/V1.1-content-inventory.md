# V1.1 Public Functionality Inventory

**Release status:** completed in September 2026.

This inventory describes the public, sanitised scope of V1.1. Production identifiers, personal data and sensitive implementation details remain in the private delivery repository.

## Acquisition and enquiry capture

- Campaign attribution captured with trial-lesson requests
- Baseline conversion event tracking
- Contact preference as structured data
- Multiple availability options
- Availability validation aligned with operational scheduling
- Existing anti-spam, validation and confirmation flow retained

## Protected administration

- Authenticated administrative access
- Enquiry list for operational follow-up
- Manual lead creation
- Status summary cards and status filters
- Progressive pagination
- Clickable lead rows and interaction refinements for repeated use

## Lead lifecycle

Supported states:

- `new`
- `contacted`
- `trial_scheduled`
- `converted`
- `trial_no_conversion`
- `archived`

The interface enforces the intended transitions between these states instead of treating status as an unrestricted label.

## Trial and follow-up handling

- Trial date required when a trial lesson is scheduled
- Trial date retained when a completed trial does not convert
- Non-converted leads can later be reactivated and scheduled again
- Internal notes preserve operational context
- First and second follow-up actions are recorded without adding artificial lifecycle states

## Measurement foundation

The release creates structured information that can support later analysis of:

- enquiry source;
- lead volume;
- contact and follow-up progression;
- trial lessons scheduled;
- conversion and non-conversion outcomes;
- demand patterns.

Advanced reporting is intentionally deferred until enough operational data exists to justify it.

## Explicitly outside V1.1

- Payment management
- Lesson and room scheduling
- Attendance and lesson summaries
- Student and teacher portals
- Newsletter automation
- Full analytics or BI dashboards
