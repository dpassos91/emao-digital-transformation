# V1.1 Project Brief

## Status

**Completed — September 2026**

V1.1 is technically and operationally closed. The release is now part of the school's normal lead-handling workflow.

## Goal

Make the V1 service measurable and easier to operate without expanding into a full school-management platform.

## Problem

V1 created a reliable digital enquiry channel, but the operational work after submission still depended on manual context: understanding how a lead preferred to be contacted, finding suitable availability, recording contact attempts, scheduling a trial lesson and retaining the final outcome.

The school also needed enough structured data to understand whether acquisition activity was producing useful enquiries and conversions.

## Delivered scope

### Lead capture and measurement

- campaign attribution stored with enquiries;
- baseline conversion tracking for the trial-lesson journey;
- structured contact preference;
- multiple availability options and supporting validation;
- form refinements based on real operational use.

### Lead-management workflow

- protected administrative interface;
- manual lead creation;
- lifecycle states for new, contacted, scheduled, converted, non-converted and archived leads;
- controlled transitions between states;
- trial-date capture when scheduling;
- retained trial history after non-conversion;
- reactivation for a later trial when appropriate;
- internal operational notes;
- first and second follow-up actions recorded within the contacted stage;
- status filtering, summary counts and progressive pagination;
- interaction improvements for repeated day-to-day use.

## Product decisions

Several decisions were intentionally process-led rather than feature-led:

- follow-up is an action within the contact process, not a separate lifecycle state;
- a completed trial without enrolment has its own outcome so it is not confused with an archived enquiry;
- trial dates are retained to preserve operational history;
- reactivation is supported because a previous non-conversion does not necessarily close the relationship permanently;
- the release stops at lead operations rather than expanding into scheduling, billing or student management.

## Acceptance outcome

The workflow was validated through practical use of the lead-management process. The release supports the operational path from enquiry to contact, trial scheduling and final outcome, while preserving enough structured history for later analysis.

## Exclusions

The following remained outside V1.1:

- payments and billing;
- timetable and room management;
- attendance and lesson summaries;
- student or teacher portals;
- newsletters and broad CRM automation;
- full reporting or business-intelligence dashboards.

## Next step

Operate the release, collect a meaningful baseline and use observed acquisition and conversion data to decide what deserves the next investment.
