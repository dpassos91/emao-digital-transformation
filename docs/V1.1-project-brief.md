# V1.1 Project Brief

## Goal
Make the V1 service measurable and easier to operate without expanding into a full management platform.

## Planned scope
Technical SEO, privacy-conscious analytics, a documented lead-management process and a protected interface for reviewing enquiries with statuses, notes, search and simple operational counts.

## Exclusions
Payments, lesson scheduling, newsletters and a full student portal remain outside V1.1.
