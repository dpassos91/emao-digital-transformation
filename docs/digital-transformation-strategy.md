# Digital Transformation Strategy

## Purpose

The EMAO website is the first product in a wider digital transformation programme. The objective is not to accumulate software features, but to use real organisational problems to improve how the school communicates, operates and makes decisions.

The programme also serves as documented evidence of end-to-end project and product leadership: from problem definition and prioritisation to delivery, adoption, measurement and iteration.

## North star

> Transform EMAO while building real, measurable and transferable evidence of the ability to lead digital transformation projects.

## Principles

1. **Start from real needs.** New work must respond to an observed user problem, operational constraint or testable opportunity.
2. **Value before functionality.** Technical interest alone is not a reason to build a feature.
3. **Solve the process first.** Confirm whether process, communication or configuration can solve the problem before adding software.
4. **Measure before expanding.** Use production behaviour, feedback and data to decide what deserves the next release.
5. **Control scope.** Separate fixes, incremental improvements, new functionality and future ideas.
6. **Keep the solution proportional.** Cost, maintenance, privacy and operational capacity must fit a small music school.
7. **Document decisions and outcomes.** Record the problem, options, decision, trade-offs, acceptance criteria, observed result and next step.

## Product progression

### V1 — public journey and structured enquiry

**Completed: 28 August 2026**

V1 established the public digital service: clear information architecture, educational offer and pricing, a structured free-trial enquiry flow, secure persistence, automated communications and production-ready infrastructure.

### V1.1 — measurement and lead operations

**Completed: September 2026**

V1.1 extended the service from enquiry capture into operational lead management. It added campaign attribution, richer contact and availability data, a protected administrative workflow, explicit lifecycle states, trial scheduling data, follow-up recording, non-conversion handling, reactivation and basic operational views.

The release deliberately stopped before becoming a full CRM or school-management system.

### Current phase — operate, measure and learn

The immediate priority after V1.1 is to use the workflow in normal operations and establish a meaningful baseline for:

- enquiry volume and source;
- time and progression through the lead process;
- trial lessons scheduled;
- conversion and non-conversion outcomes;
- demand by instrument or learning profile;
- operational friction identified through repeated use.

### Future releases

Possible areas include deeper reporting, attendance and lesson-summary workflows, communication improvements and selected scheduling support.

These are opportunities, not committed scope. Each should pass a fresh discovery and prioritisation step before implementation.

## Decision framework

Before starting a new initiative, assess:

- the concrete problem being solved;
- the user or operator who benefits;
- expected organisational impact;
- evidence supporting the priority;
- implementation and maintenance effort;
- privacy, security and operational risk;
- available capacity;
- how success will be measured.

Work should be prioritised when organisational value and demonstrable project-management value reinforce each other.

## Portfolio evidence

The programme should continue to document:

- context and problem definition;
- stakeholders and requirements;
- scope and exclusions;
- milestones and acceptance criteria;
- critical decisions and trade-offs;
- implementation and validation;
- adoption and operational change;
- quantitative and qualitative results;
- lessons learned and subsequent iterations.

The case study should distinguish clearly between leadership, product decisions, coordination, AI-assisted implementation and observed outcomes.
