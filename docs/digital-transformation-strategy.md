# Digital Transformation Strategy

The website is the first product in a wider programme to make EMAO's services easier to understand, access and operate.

## Principles
- Start from real user and operational needs.
- Deliver in small, testable releases.
- Keep data collection purposeful and secure.
- Document decisions, scope and acceptance criteria.
- Build foundations for measurement and continuous improvement.

## Progression
V1 establishes the public journey and structured enquiry flow. V1.1 focuses on measurement and lead operations. Later releases may address communication, scheduling and student-service workflows only when justified by validated needs.
